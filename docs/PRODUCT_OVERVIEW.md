# Product Overview

## Summary

Status: Observed

The reference product is a creator growth tool for gated links. Public pages describe a product where visitors complete one or more actions before a destination link or file unlocks. The public site also advertises link analytics, email capture, integrations, and link-in-bio.

Evidence:

- `evidence/screenshots/public/homepage-desktop.png`
- `evidence/notes/public-homepage-desktop.json`
- `evidence/screenshots/public/public-link-01-how-it-works.png`

## Target Users

Status: Inferred from public content

- Content creators who share downloads, presets, snippets, private communities, or promotional resources.
- YouTubers, streamers, music producers, template creators, and community builders.
- Users who want followers, subscribers, email contacts, or community joins in exchange for access.

## Free-Scope Product Positioning

Status: Observed

Public copy says links can be created without a card and viewers do not need an account. The public create page says the link will be saved to the user's account when they sign up.

Evidence:

- `evidence/screenshots/create-link/public-create-initial.png`
- `evidence/notes/interaction-create-public.json`

## Excluded From Rebuild Scope

Status: Required

Do not include billing, subscriptions, Pro plan, checkout, paid entitlements, or paid-only customization in the rebuild specification. Pricing links were visible in the footer, but were not researched beyond noting their presence.
