How it works

Turn every link you share into growth.

Add an unlock step to anything you share. Your audience watches, follows, joins, or signs up, and your content unlocks for them automatically.

Three steps. About a minute.

01 / Create

Build a link

Paste a URL, upload a file, or write a snippet. Pick the social actions visitors need to complete (follow, subscribe, join, like) and publish.

02 / Share

Drop it anywhere

Put your Rekonise link in your bio, tweet, video description, or group chat. Anyone who clicks lands on a clean unlock page. No account required.

03 / Grow

Watch it work

As visitors complete the steps, your channels grow on autopilot. Your dashboard shows exactly which links convert and where the traffic comes from.

Common questions

Everything visitors and creators tend to ask.

Do my viewers need a Rekonise account to unlock content?

No. Visitors click your link, complete the social actions on the original platform (YouTube, Discord, X, etc.), and your content unlocks for them. They never have to sign up to Rekonise.

 How do you verify the actions are actually completed?

We check each action against the source platform (verified subscribes, follows, joins, and so on) via official APIs and account-linked verification. There's no way to skip the step.

 What kinds of content can I gate behind a link?

Any URL (Google Drive, a YouTube video, an external download), uploaded files (PDFs, ZIPs, images), or text snippets like access codes, coupons, or messages.

 Will my audience get spammed?

No. Rekonise never emails or notifies your viewers. Any data they share (like an email captured during unlock) goes only to you, in your dashboard.

 What happens if a platform changes their API?

Our team monitors every supported platform actively. When verification changes, we ship updates fast so your existing links keep working without you needing to do anything.

 Can I see who clicked but didn't complete the steps?

Yes. Analytics show views, unlocks, and conversion rate per link. You can see where traffic drops off and tune your actions accordingly.
